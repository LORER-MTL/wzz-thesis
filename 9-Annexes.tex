\Annexe{BENCHMARK PROBLEMS}\label{app:bench}

Table~\ref{tab:instances-desc} describes the $25$ problems of the benchmark
suite~\cite{cvxpybenchmarks} used in Chapter~\ref{sec:Theme2}, and
Table~\ref{tab:instances} gives their dimensions, both in the order of
Table~\ref{tab:oneshot-compile}. The dimensions are those of the problem data that
\texttt{diffengine} returns for \texttt{Clarabel}: the matrix
$\mmat{A} \in \reals^{m \times n}$ of the constraints and the matrix $\mmat{P}$ of the
objective, both counted after canonicalization, so $n$ includes the auxiliary variables of
the reductions. The density is the share of nonzero entries of $\mmat{A}$,
$\mathrm{nnz}(\mmat{A})/(mn)$, the column ``param.\ size'' is the total number of entries of
the parameters of the problem, and the last column says whether the problem is \ac{DPP}. A dash
denotes a problem without a quadratic objective or without parameters.

SVMWithL1Regularization & Hinge-loss classifier with an $\ell_1$ penalty, $25\,000$ samples and $500$ features. The penalty weight is a parameter. \\
FactorCovarianceModel & Factor-model portfolio selection with $25\,000$ assets and $250$ factors. The risk aversion and the leverage limit are parameters. \\
Yitzhaki & Gini mean-difference portfolio \ac{LP} over a dense matrix of all $44\,850$ pairwise return differences of $50$ assets. \\
SimpleQP & \ac{QP} with a dense $1600 \times 1600$ quadratic form, $8000$ inequality and $20$ equality constraints. \\
HuberRegression & Huber regression with $3000$ features and $4500$ samples, $12\,\%$ of the labels flipped. \\
LeastSquares & Dense least squares, $\lVert \mmat{A}\mvec{x} - \mvec{b} \rVert_2^2$ with $\mmat{A} \in \reals^{7000 \times 2000}$. \\
SimpleLP & Box-constrained \ac{LP} with $10^7$ variables and a linear objective. \\
SimpleScalarParametrizedLP & Box-constrained \ac{LP} with $2 \times 10^6$ variables whose cost vector is scaled by a scalar parameter. \\
SimpleFullyParametrizedLP & Box-constrained \ac{LP} with $10^6$ variables whose cost vector is a parameter. \\
SlowPruning & Matrix least squares with a $100 \times 20$ variable and dense data, from \texttt{CVXPY} issue 1668. \\
ParametrizedQP & Box-constrained least squares whose $6000 \times 2400$ matrix and right-hand side are parameters. \\
Cajas & Gini portfolio \ac{LP} in ordered-weighted-average form, with $800$ vector constraints added one by one. \\
ConeMatrixStuffing & The \ac{LP} of SmallMatrixStuffing at size $5000$. \\
SmallMatrixStuffing & \ac{LP} with $4000$ variables and $4000$ scalar constraints, each a separate constraint object. \\
TvInpainting & Total-variation inpainting of a $512 \times 512$ colour image, with about $30\,\%$ of the pixels known. \\
ParamSmallMatrixStuffing & The \ac{LP} of SmallMatrixStuffing at size $300$, with its matrix, constraint coefficients and right-hand side as parameters. \\
ParamConeMatrixStuffing & The same parametric \ac{LP} at size $200$. \\
SemidefiniteProgramming & Standard-form \ac{SDP} with a $200 \times 200$ variable and $120$ dense equality constraints. \\
CVaR & Conditional value-at-risk \ac{LP} over a dense matrix of $131\,072$ scenarios and $768$ variables. \\
QuantumHilbertMatrix & \ac{SDP} with partial traces and partial transposes of a $64 \times 64$ positive semidefinite variable, with sparse constants. \\
Murray & The Yitzhaki \ac{LP} written with a dense $244\,650 \times 700$ matrix of pairwise differences. \\
SDPSegfault1132 & Fits a distance matrix through a $99 \times 99$ positive semidefinite Gram matrix and dense constants, from \texttt{CVXPY} issue 1132. \\
OptimalAdvertising & Allocation of $250$ advertisers over $1000$ time slots, with one minimum term per advertiser. \\
ConvexPlasticity & One step of a convex plasticity model at $3000$ points, with a von Mises yield criterion and three parameters. \\
UnconstrainedQP & Fits a complex signal through a $252 \times 252$ discrete Fourier transform matrix and a Kronecker product with a diagonal of the variable, from \texttt{CVXPY} issue 2205. Despite its name, a second-order cone problem. \\
